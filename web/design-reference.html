03 · Header, navigation and Settings

Two destinations, Gatherings (Gathering, Campaign, Roster) and Cards (Library, Print queue), addressed by hash routes (#gatherings, #gatherings/campaign, #gatherings/roster, #gatherings/roster/cards, #cards, #cards/queue). The open destination is a raised panel with a border; no underline. Language and Theme live in the Settings menu. One primary action: Print, which names what it prints.

04 · Controls

Selects are themed dropdowns on fine pointers (web/js/select.js, a WAI-ARIA select-only combobox over the native <select>, which stays the source of truth) and the native picker, restyled, on touch. Long lists get a filter field. An option may carry data-reason: shown muted under it and announced as its description; it stays selectable. Affinities are visible radios. Icons are Lucide, from web/js/lucide.js.

06 · The "Add" picker

Each list on a model card has its own Add button, opening one shared modal <dialog> (web/js/builder-picker.js): a search over name, kind and rules text, filter chips by kind, and the rows grouped as on the cards with cost and a short summary. A row that would break a rule says why (data-reason, the first new warning from the real rules engine) and still adds: the Builder warns, it never blocks. In a campaign, "From the stash (free)" comes before "Buy at the Markets". Adding keeps it open ("Added Blade."); Done closes it. Full screen on phones.
